Shelbot

A game guide that lives inside this site. Shelbot knows the six phases, the five roles, the four city indicators, and all 184 cards. Ask how it plays, look up a card, or ask about Samarinda's sustainability issues. Shelbot+ answers more flexibly with the help of a language model; you can pick the model or compare them all at once. If you choose “Script”, everything runs in your browser.

Shelbot guides away from the table — for learning before a session and looking back after one. During play the game's own rules stand: GenAI use is rationed through GenAI Access Tokens, and the decision belongs to the five roles.

Shelbot

Shelbot+ · Llama 3.3 70B, built on the script

Hello! I'm Shelbot. I know this game inside out — its six phases, five roles, four city indicators, and all 184 cards. Ask me anything, or start with one of these.

Normal mode answers from what the game already says. Shelbot+ uses a language model and can be wrong, so check important numbers with your facilitator or teacher. Decisions at the table stay with you and your group.

The limits Shelbot keeps

  • Answers come from the official guide, the site text, and the 184 cards. Nothing is invented.

  • Shelbot holds no vote at the table. It does not price projects and does not pick priorities.

  • Normal mode answers from the script only. When a question falls outside the game, it says it does not know.

  • Shelbot+ uses the language model you pick (Llama on Cloudflare, or Claude by Anthropic when available), builds on the script answer, and may discuss Samarinda issues such as mining, the river, and flooding. Questions outside the game's world are still declined.

  • In Shelbot+, questions are sent to be answered and we do not store them. With “Script”, nothing is sent to a server.

  • GenAI use inside the game is a separate matter, with its own rules in the guide.

Inside the game, two core uses are free: the system map in Phase 1 and the impact simulation in Phase 4. Further prompts cost a GenAI Access Token, earned through verification, bias detection, local knowledge, or well-designed prompting.

Phase 2 studio · Imagine futures

Draw your Samarinda 2045

Pick one of the three scenarios, then write what you imagine: the river, the streets, the neighbourhoods, the work. Shelbot turns it into an illustration. The picture is a prompt for discussion, not a forecast, and it is always marked as AI-made.

The way the city works is rearranged, not patched.

You may leave it empty: Shelbot uses this scenario's default vision.0/400

Your text is sent to Cloudflare to be checked and drawn. Images are not stored. Limit of three images per minute. Do not write people's names or personal data.
