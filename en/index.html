What will Samarinda look like in 2045? A greener, safer, fairer city, or one facing steadily harder environmental problems? This game asks you to weigh four forces that pull against each other, and to decide together with other people.

01 / 06Phase 2 · Imagine futures

Every table builds four Samarindas, then picks one

A few decisions go differently, and the direction shifts.

The second scenario changes some choices, not the whole system. What it tests is simple: which decisions move the most if taken earlier, and who carries the consequences. Most groups find this future the easiest to picture, and for that reason the easiest to argue about.

In Phase 3, one of the four is chosen. The choice only stands with at least four of the five roles behind it, and no critical harm left unaddressed.

04 / 06About

What this actually is

A physical board game

Five players, six phases, 184 cards, and a board of eight thematic city zones. Played on a table, not on a screen.

Real Samarinda issues

Flooding, waste and river pollution, abandoned mining pits, and shrinking green space. Not an invented setting.

GenAI inside the game

Shelbot is the GenAI used inside this board game. At the table it serves as a thinking partner: mapping causes and effects, comparing options, and helping players imagine futures. Its use is rationed through GenAI Access Tokens, and it holds no vote in the decision.

05 / 06The arc of play

Six phases, from reading the city to acting beyond the table

  1. 1

    Observe the Present

    A system map: understanding the city and how its problems interlock.

  2. 2

    Imagine Futures

    Four scenarios: Expected, Alternative, Preferred, and Transformative Future.

  3. 3

    Choose a Future

    One scenario is chosen. It passes with at least 4 of 5 roles behind it and no unaddressed critical harm.

  4. 4

    Make Decisions

    Actions are chosen, risks weighed, and impacts simulated.

  5. 5

    Act Together

    Three projects reach Committed status, with their network of backers mapped.

  6. 6

    Real Impact

    A simulated impact profile, then one real commitment outside the game.
