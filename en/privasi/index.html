Privacy

What this site keeps, and what it does not

This page is short and plain. It is not a general promise; it describes what the site actually does when you open it.

In shortfour lines
  • 01No accounts and no forms.
  • 02No analytics and no advertising cookies.
  • 03The score is computed in your browser. Shelbot+ and the Phase 2 image studio send your text to Cloudflare; in Shelbot, choose “Script” to answer in your browser.
  • 04The only stored thing is your theme choice.

01 / 02The detail

  • ✓

    Your theme choice

    If you switch between light and dark, that choice is stored in your own browser under the key sf-theme. It is never sent anywhere, and it disappears when you clear site data.

  • ✓

    The Shelbot conversation

    In normal mode, Shelbot runs entirely in your browser. Its answers come from the site text, the game guide, and the 184 cards that ship with the page. No question is sent to a server, and the conversation is gone once you close the page.

  • !

    Shelbot+

    Shelbot+ is on from the moment the page opens. While it is on, your question, the chat history on that page, and the script answer are sent to our service on Cloudflare to be answered by the model you pick: Llama (Cloudflare Workers AI), or Claude when available, which is passed on to Anthropic. “Compare all” sends the same question to all of those models. We do not store that conversation. To limit abuse, the service counts questions per IP address over one minute, without storing them. Choose “Script” if you do not want anything sent. Do not write personal data in the chat.

  • !

    The Phase 2 image studio

    When you press “Draw my vision”, the chosen scenario and your text are sent to our service on Cloudflare. The text is checked by Llama Guard, turned into a prompt by a Llama model, then drawn by the Flux model (all Cloudflare Workers AI). Neither the image nor your text is stored. Images are counted per IP address over one minute, without being stored. Images are always marked as AI-made and are not photos of Samarinda. In “Predict the future” mode, the scenario and the three boxes (past, today's trends, actions) are sent the same way, checked by Llama Guard, and answered by a Llama model. Neither your entries nor the prediction are stored.

  • ✓

    Your mini game score

    The score is counted in your browser and never stored. The share button only acts when you press it: it hands one sentence with your score and the page address to your device's share sheet, or copies it to your clipboard.

  • ✕

    No analytics

    There is no Google Analytics, no tracking pixel, and no advertising cookie. This site does not follow where you go after you close the page.

  • ✕

    No third-party requests

    The Fraunces and Inter typefaces are served from this site's own server, not from Google. Illustrations are drawn as SVG inside the page. Opening this page therefore does not tell any third party that you opened it.

  • ✕

    No sign-up form

    There is no account, no name field, and no email field on this site. Getting in touch runs through WhatsApp or email, both of which open in your own app.

  • !

    What we do not control

    The site is served by a hosting provider, and providers like that normally record IP addresses and page requests in their own server logs. Those logs belong to the provider, not to us, and we do not read them.

02 / 02Going forward

If this changes

The site is still growing. If another part of it starts sending data out of your browser, this page will be updated before that change is switched on.
